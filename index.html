FAQ

Before you grab the mic.

What are your hours?

We're open Monday–Friday from 6:00 PM to 2:00 AM, and Saturday–Sunday from 3:00 PM to 2:00 AM. The kitchen stays open late — last orders at 1:30 AM.

How big are your private rooms?

We offer private rooms of various sizes, accommodating small gatherings all the way up to private parties of 50 people. Our dining area can also be converted into a VIP karaoke room upon request.

Is Moxie a restaurant too?

Yes — we are more than just a karaoke venue. We are a Japanese restaurant specializing in sushi and grills, with a full food and drinks menu.

What kind of food do you serve?

Our Japanese Izakaya menu features fresh sushi and sashimi, our signature Uni Udon, ramen, takoyaki, grilled pork jowl, and desserts like warabi mochi — enjoyed in our dining area or right in your private karaoke room.

Where is Moxie Lounge & Karaoke located?

We're at 1205 S Baldwin Avenue #A, Arcadia, California 91007 — minutes from Pasadena, Monrovia, and Temple City, and an easy drive from anywhere in the San Gabriel Valley or greater Los Angeles.

Do you have a space for watching live sports?

Yes! We feature a dedicated Live TV Sports Dining Room with a massive TV wall. Enjoy your favorite games, major sporting events, and hockey playoffs in a vibrant atmosphere while indulging in our premium Japanese Izakaya cuisine.

Can I watch World Cup 2026 matches at Moxie?

Yes! We host World Cup 2026 viewing parties in our Live TV Sports Dining Room with a massive TV wall. Catch every match with premium Japanese Izakaya food and drinks — book a table or reserve a private room for your own watch party.

What types of events can I host at Moxie?

We're a top-rated choice for private events and social celebrations of every kind — corporate socials, birthday parties, family reunions, client appreciation dinners, and lively watch parties. With private rooms, professional catering, and entertainment all under one roof, we offer a turn-key solution that keeps your event seamless, engaging, and memorable.

Is Moxie a good venue for birthday parties?

Absolutely. Our private rooms fit everything from small get-togethers up to 50 guests, and our automatic cotton candy machine is always a hit. Add a full food and drinks menu plus state-of-the-art karaoke, and you have a turn-key birthday celebration.

What is KTV-style private karaoke?

KTV (karaoke television) means singing in your own private room with friends instead of on a public stage — the style popular across Asia. Every room at Moxie has its own state-of-the-art karaoke system, so your group controls the playlist, the volume, and the vibe.

How does the Happy Hour Special work?

It's $30 per person for the entire duration. This charge includes complimentary food and drinks valued up to $30 from our menu. Available Monday–Thursday 6 PM–9 PM, Friday 6 PM–8 PM, and Saturday–Sunday 3 PM–7 PM.

Do you serve drinks?

Yes — we offer a full drinks menu alongside our Japanese food menu, plus daily Happy Hour specials. Non-drinkers are just as welcome, with plenty to enjoy alcohol-free.

Do I have to drink to have a good time?

Absolutely not. We cater to all preferences — whether you're enjoying a drink or embracing the new wave of sober culture. It's a welcoming and balanced environment for everyone.

Is Moxie family friendly?

Yes. Moxie is an ideal spot for family gatherings, where guests of all ages can enjoy an unforgettable experience.

Can I order to-go?

Yes — we're on DoorDash, Uber Eats, and GrubHub for delivery and pickup.

How does the service charge work?

Moxie is fully compliant with California's SB 1524 regulations. An 18% service charge is applied to all bookings and is clearly disclosed in advance during the online booking process. Upon check-in, we also send a text message to your contact number outlining our full pricing and service charge policy, so every guest has an immediate, written record of all costs.

How do I make a reservation?

Book directly through our Reservations page — you can secure your room online in just a few clicks. You can also call us at 626-525-8889 or email info@moxiekaraoke.com. Weekends book up fast — reserve early!
